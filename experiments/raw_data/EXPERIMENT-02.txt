EXPERIMENT-02

Date: 14 July 2026
Controller: Raspberry Pi Pico 2W
Firmware: MicroPython
Motor Driver: L298N
Pressure Sensor: BMP280
PWM Frequency: 1000 Hz

OBJECTIVE:
To repeat the PWM–vacuum characterization and evaluate the
repeatability of pump operation at high PWM duty cycle.

PNEUMATIC CONFIGURATION:
Pneumatic test configuration used during repeated PWM testing.

OBSERVATIONS:
- Vacuum initially increased during 100% PWM actuation.
- Repeated loss and recovery of pump actuation was physically observed.
- The pressure signal showed repeated vacuum build-up followed by
  pressure recovery toward atmospheric pressure.
- The pump subsequently restarted and vacuum generation resumed.
- The instability was most prominent during 100% PWM operation.

IMPORTANT:
The exact electrical cause of the intermittent actuation was not
confirmed during this test.

POSSIBLE CONTRIBUTING FACTORS:
- Battery state of charge
- Voltage drop across the L298N motor driver
- Power-delivery limitation under high motor load
- Pneumatic loading and leakage in the temporary test arrangement

INTERPRETATION:
The repeated pressure recovery events during a continuous 100% PWM
software command indicated intermittent loss of effective pneumatic
actuation. This test highlighted the need to evaluate both the
electrical power-delivery path and the pneumatic configuration.
