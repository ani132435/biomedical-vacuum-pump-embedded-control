EXPERIMENTAL TEST SUMMARY
Biomimetic Breast Pump Embedded Control Prototype
Date: 14 July 2026

TEST 01:
Initial PWM–vacuum characterization.

80% PWM:
Average vacuum = -2.31 mmHg
Maximum vacuum = -2.46 mmHg

100% PWM:
Average vacuum = -3.99 mmHg
Maximum vacuum = -5.31 mmHg


TEST 02:
Repeated high-PWM test.

Observation:
Intermittent pump actuation loss and recovery observed during
100% PWM operation.

Pressure response:
Repeated vacuum build-up followed by recovery toward atmospheric
pressure and subsequent vacuum generation.


TEST 03:
Direct pump-to-BMP280 chamber characterization.

Atmospheric baseline:
705.02 mmHg

80% PWM:
Average vacuum = -2.91 mmHg
Maximum vacuum = -3.08 mmHg

100% PWM:
Average vacuum = -4.28 mmHg
Maximum vacuum = -5.75 mmHg

Observation:
Stable pump operation throughout the test.
Direct pneumatic configuration produced a cleaner pressure response.

PRIMARY CHARACTERIZATION RESULT:
Test 03 is considered the primary PWM–vacuum characterization test.

MAIN LIMITATION:
The maximum measured pressure differential remained below the
intended vacuum range for the final breast-pump application.

PROPOSED NEXT DEVELOPMENT:
- Improved pump power-delivery architecture
- Appropriate vacuum/differential pressure sensor
- Pneumatic sealing improvement
- Active venting using a solenoid valve
- Closed-loop pressure regulation
