# Biomimetic Suction Notes

## Motivation

Conventional breast pumps primarily rely on constant vacuum suction.

Natural breastfeeding consists of rhythmic, pulsatile pressure cycles that vary in both frequency and amplitude.

---

## Operating Modes

| Mode | Approx. Vacuum | Relative Frequency |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| Stimulation | -50 to -100 mmHg | High |
| Expression | -100 to -200 mmHg | Lower |

---

## Design Goals

- Mimic natural infant suckling.
- Implement two operating modes.
- Use closed-loop pressure feedback.
- Compensate for leakage and tissue variability.

---

## Research Hypothesis

A biomimetic vacuum waveform generated using a PWM-controlled diaphragm pump and a solenoid valve can improve the similarity between mechanical pumping and natural infant suckling.
